01 • The Challenge

THE PROBLEM

Freshly collected milk is highly perishable and needs rapid chilling after collection. When milk remains at normal temperature for too long, microbial growth can increase and milk quality can deteriorate.

QUALITY RISK
01

Delayed Chilling

Milk may remain warm immediately after collection.

02

First-Mile Transportation

Milk often has to travel from collection points to chilling facilities.

03

Temperature Rise

Ambient heat during transportation can increase milk temperature.

04

Limited Local Cooling

Conventional chilling infrastructure may not be available at every collection point.

Our goal: Bring active chilling closer to the point of milk collection.

Source note: “Raw milk requires immediate chilling to preserve quality.” Reference: NDDB.

Core idea: The can is docked onto the cooling base for active chilling. The refrigeration system transfers cooling through the thermal interface. PCM stores cooling energy during the chilling stage. Once the target temperature is reached, the can can be detached and transported while insulation and PCM help retain the low temperature.

03 • Process

HOW IT WORKS

Click any step to highlight the operation.

STEP 01

COLLECT

Fresh milk is collected and filled into the 40 L SS304 can.

STEP 02

DOCK

The filled can is positioned onto the cooling base.

STEP 03

LOCK

The quick-lock mechanism secures the can and establishes the thermal connection.

STEP 04

ACTIVE COOLING

The compressor-based refrigeration system starts removing heat.

STEP 05

HEAT TRANSFER

Cooling passes through the thermal interface and spreading layer toward the milk.

STEP 06

PCM CHARGING

PCM stores cooling energy during active chilling.

STEP 07

TARGET TEMPERATURE

The system works toward the target range of approximately 4–8°C.

STEP 08

DETACH & TRANSPORT

The can is detached and transported while temperature continues to be monitored.

05 • Thermal System

OUR COOLING TECHNOLOGY

The active cooling path uses a compressor-based refrigeration system. PCM supports temperature maintenance and does not replace refrigeration.

Technical principle: The compressor-based refrigeration system performs the active cooling. The PCM does not replace the refrigeration system; it stores cooling energy and supports temperature maintenance after active chilling. The refrigeration circuit remains sealed and isolated from the milk.

The temperature sensor continuously monitors milk temperature. The ultrasonic sensor estimates milk level. The MPU6050 detects tilt. The ESP32 processes the sensor data and controls cooling according to the defined temperature logic.

07 • Control Logic

AUTOMATIC TEMPERATURE CONTROL

TEMPERATURE ABOVE TARGET
→
COOLING ON
TARGET TEMPERATURE REACHED
→
COOLING REDUCED / OFF
TEMPERATURE RISES
→
COOLING ON AGAIN
The system avoids unnecessary continuous compressor operation by using temperature-based control. This supports lower energy consumption while maintaining the required temperature range. No measured energy-saving percentage is claimed.
